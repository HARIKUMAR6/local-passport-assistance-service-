Replacement, Lost/Damaged & Correction

When something goes wrong, we move quickly.

Lost or stolen

Urgent
  1. Secure your identity and report the loss.
  2. We prepare your replacement application.
  3. We guide urgent travel options.

Damaged

Common
  1. Check whether it is still valid for travel.
  2. We prepare supporting statements.
  3. Submit the replacement with guidance.

Correction

Detail fix
  1. Identify the incorrect detail.
  2. Gather official supporting evidence.
  3. Submit a clean correction request.
