Minor Passport Documents

Passports for children and teens.

Rules for minors differ widely between countries. These are the topics most authorities cover.

Requirements vary by country and passport authority. These are typical documents only; confirm the exact list with your authority.

01Parent or guardian consent

Many authorities require consent from one or both parents or legal guardians. Some also ask for the guardian's own identity documents.

02Proof of relationship

A birth certificate, or adoption or guardianship papers, usually shows the link between the child and the applying adult.

03The child's identity

Evidence of the child's identity and nationality, such as a birth certificate or an existing ID, is commonly requested.

04Photos and attendance

Photo rules still apply, though some authorities relax them for infants. Some require the child to attend in person.
